## Standard Attack Rules

### Attack Types

- **Melee**: Attack enemies in the same Zone.
- **Ranged**: Attack enemies in Zones you can see and are within range.
 

### Attack Steps

1. **Select Attack**  
    Choose an attack from your Identity Card.

2. **Choose Target Zone**  
    Must be in range and within Line of Sight.

3. **Roll Dice**  
    - Roll the attack's dice.
    - Add dice equal to your Hunger.
    - Add bonuses from Traits, Bystanders, or abilities.

4. **Check Successes**  
    Each die ≥ Accuracy is 1 Success.

5. **Assign Successes**  
    Use this order:
    1. Superhero
    2. Guard
    3. Soldier
    4. Specialist
    5. Bystander *(Devour only)*
    
    A target is eliminated if it takes Successes equal to its Toughness in one attack.
 
### Notes

- Extra successes go to the next target in order.
- Partial damage does **not** carry over.
- After the attack, for each **⚡** rolled, increase Hunger by **1**.
