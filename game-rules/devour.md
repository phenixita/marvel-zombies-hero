# Devour

## What is Devour?

**Devour** is a special **Melee Attack** available to all Zombie Heroes in Marvel Zombies.

- Functions like a normal melee attack, but with key differences:
    - **Can target Bystanders** (unlike regular attacks).
    - After a successful Devour (eliminating the target), the Zombie Hero **resets their Hunger to 0**.
    - **Only one target** can be eliminated per Devour attack, even if you roll extra successes.

Devour is essential for managing Hunger. Hunger increases every round and with attacks. If it reaches the maximum (4), the Zombie Hero becomes **Ravenous**—limiting actions and causing damage at turn end.

## How to Use Devour

1. **Choose Devour as your attack action** during your turn.
2. **Target a figure in your Zone** (Devour is melee, so range = 0).
3. **Roll dice** for Devour, plus extra dice equal to your current Hunger level.
4. **Apply successes** in this priority order:
        1. Superhero
        2. Guard
        3. Soldier
        4. Specialist
        5. Bystander _(only Devour can target Bystanders)_
5. **If the attack eliminates the target:**
        - **Reset Hunger to 0** immediately.
        - If the target was a Bystander, take its card and gain its special ability.
6. **Note:** Excess successes are lost—Devour only eliminates one target per attack.

## Quick Reference

- **Devour = Melee attack**
- **Targets Bystanders**
- **Resets Hunger to 0 on success**
- **One target per attack**
- **Essential for Hunger management**
